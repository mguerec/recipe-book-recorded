# Fried Beef
## Ingredients:
- Minced beef
- 1 onion
- 3 cloves of garlic
- Olive oil
- Can of Black beans
- Spices: Cumin, Cinnamon, Paprika, Oregano, Thyme

## Instructions: 
- Dice the onion, crush the garlic and fry in olive oil
- Add lots of spices to the frying onions for 30 seconds
- Leave the spiced onions to cool
- Drain, rinse, and mash the beans with a fork
- Fry the beef with plenty of salt
- Mix together
