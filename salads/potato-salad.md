# Potato salad recipe


## Ingredients

- 3 potatoes
- 1 cup of Mayonnaise
- 3 pickles
- salt and pepper


## Instructions

1. **Boil the potatoes:**
   - Peel the potatoes.
   - Boil them for 20 minutes.

2. **Smash the potatoes:**
   - smash the potatoes to become uniform.

3. **Mix:**
   - Add mayonnaise, salt an pepper.

4. **Garnish (Optional):**
   - Chop the pickles and put on the top

5. **Serve:**
   - Enjoy the Potato salad as is, or chill it in the refrigerator for about an hour before serving.
